# Essay scene illustrations

Three original scene backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The fourth scene reuses the original rain-cleared cherry-town illustration documented in essay-cherry-rain-artwork.md. The printed user photograph is not included.

## essay-garden

Original landscape illustration for an introspective personal essay. Hand-painted Japanese animation background of an empty wooden pavilion in a lush Japanese garden during gentle summer rain. Rainwater ripples, mossy stone path, vivid but soft green leaves, dappled light, quiet tenderness. No people, no text, no logos. Wide panoramic 3:1 composition, pavilion and foliage clustered on the left, calm softly lit space on the right. Detailed painterly landscape with mist-green and muted blue palette. No poster design.
+## essay-meeting

Original hand-painted Japanese animation landscape for a personal essay about encounters and memory. Quiet small-town hilltop stairway at twilight, railings and rooftops, warm windows, lavender blue sky with a delicate distant comet trail, soft golden horizon. No people, no text, no logos. Panoramic 3:1, detailed atmospheric landscape, architecture mostly on the right, calm open sky on the left. Tender hopeful mood, pale blue, lavender and peach, stylistically consistent with a painted cherry-blossom town after rain.
+## essay-platform

Original hand-painted Japanese animation panoramic landscape of a quiet empty railway platform beneath pale pink cherry blossom trees after rain, at dawn. Tracks recede into mist, a few drifting petals, puddles reflect luminous pale sky, a simple station bench, soft blue-gray and cherry-pink color palette. No people, no trains, no text, no logos. Wide 3:1 composition, rail tracks and blossoms on the left, clean softly lit atmospheric space on the right, detailed painterly scenery, gentle hopeful mood.
