I worked on this Long March website for an undergraduate innovation project proposal. The route is the main way into the site: pick a stop and read about what happened there and who was involved.

## Route and source material

The site combines route-based reading with a knowledge base. Questions can be answered with material retrieved from the collection, and the dialogue and narration connect people and events to specific stops. Quizzes and story scenes give visitors another way to go through the material.

## Running the site

The site is built in Streamlit. The repository documents how to start it locally and which model services need credentials. The route and written material can be browsed on their own; features that call a model need a configured endpoint.
